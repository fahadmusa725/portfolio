## Overview
DineFlow is a multi-tenant restaurant POS, kitchen display and inventory platform. One deployment serves many restaurants, and each restaurant only sees its own data.

Staff take orders at the POS terminal, the kitchen sees them on a live screen, waiters are told when food is ready, and every sale updates inventory, customer credit and reports. A platform owner creates restaurants, picks a plan (Basic or Pro) and sets the trial length. Each restaurant then manages its own menu, tables, staff, customers, stock and reports.

## Architecture
All restaurants share one database and the same collections, and every restaurant document carries a `restaurantId`.

After authentication, a `setTenant` middleware reads the `restaurantId` from the JWT and sets `req.tenantId`. It also checks the restaurant's status, so a suspended restaurant, or one whose trial has ended, gets a 403. Controllers then query with `restaurantId: req.tenantId`.

Authentication is a JWT with a seven-day expiry, carrying the user id, role and `restaurantId`. Six roles exist: `super-admin`, `restaurant-admin`, `manager`, `cashier`, `waiter` and `kitchen`. Route-level `authorize(...roles)` checks the role, and `checkPermission(module)` checks the restaurant's plan and the staff member's custom module permissions.

Realtime runs on Ably. The backend publishes order events to a restaurant-specific channel, and the browser subscribes with a short-lived, subscribe-only token limited to its own restaurant's channel.

## Key decisions

<Decision title="The server verifies everything the browser sends" why="Cart totals, coupons, tax and stock can be tampered with in the browser, so the backend validates stock and recalculates prices, discounts, coupons and tax when an order is created.">
Orders are rejected when there is not enough stock to make them.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Access control has two layers on top of roles" why="Fixed roles alone are too rigid. Layering the plan tier and per-staff permissions lets Basic and Pro plans differ, and lets a cashier get access to one specific report.">
Module permissions start from the role defaults, and the restaurant admin can adjust them per account.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Stock deduction is atomic and reversible" why="Selling an item deducts its recipe ingredients, so a failed order must not leave stock wrongly reduced.">
Deduction is atomic and is undone if the order fails to save or is cancelled. Cancelling also restores the customer's credit.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Realtime access is scoped per restaurant" why="A realtime channel is another way to leak data between tenants, so tokens are short lived and subscribe-only, limited to the user's own restaurant channel.">
The backend publishes events. Browsers can only listen.
</Decision>

## Current limitations
- There is no online payment gateway or automatic subscription billing. Plans and trials are managed by the super admin.
- There is no seed script. The first restaurant and its data are created through the app.
